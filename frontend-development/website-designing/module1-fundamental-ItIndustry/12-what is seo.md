What is SEO? List out advantages of SEO.

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization.
It is the process of improving a website so that it appears higher in search engine results like Google, Bing, and Yahoo when people search for related information.

Example:
If someone searches “best jewellery shop in Rajkot”, SEO helps a jewellery website appear on the first page of search results.

Advantages of SEO

1. Increases Website Traffic
More people visit the website through search engines.

2. Improves Website Ranking
Helps the website appear higher in search results.

3. Builds Brand Awareness
People recognize and trust websites that appear at the top.

4. Cost-Effective Marketing
SEO brings organic (free) traffic without paying for ads regularly.

5. Better User Experience
SEO improves website speed, design, and mobile friendliness.

6. Increases Sales and Customers
More visitors can lead to more business and profit.

7. 24/7 Promotion
A website can appear in search results anytime, day or night.

8. Helps Beat Competitors
Better SEO can rank a website above competitor websites.

9. Long-Term Results
Good SEO can give benefits for a long time.

10. Targets the Right Audience
SEO helps attract users searching for specific products or services.
