What is Google Business Manager?

Google Business Manager usually refers to Google Business Profile (earlier called Google My Business). It is a free tool by Google that helps businesses appear on:

Google Search
Google Maps


Businesses can use it to:
Add business name, address, and phone number
Show opening hours
Upload photos/videos
Reply to customer reviews
Share updates and offers
Help customers find the business online

Example: If someone searches “jewellery shop near me” on Google, businesses with Google Business Profiles appear in the results.


Steps to Add Business on Google
Step 1: Open Google Business Website

Go to:

step 1: Google Business Profile

Step 2: Sign In

Sign in using your Google/Gmail account.

Step 3: Click “Manage Now”

Press the Manage Now button.

Step 4: Enter Business Name

Type your business name.

If your business already exists on Google, select it.
Otherwise click “Add your business to Google.”

Step 5: Select Business Category

Choose the correct category like:

Jewellery Shop
Restaurant
Clothing Store
Coaching Class
Salon, etc.

Step 6: Add Business Location

Enter:

Shop address
City
PIN code

This helps your business appear on Google Maps.

Step 7: Add Contact Details

Add:

Phone number
Website link (optional)

Step 8: Verify Your Business

Google will verify ownership through:

OTP call
SMS
Email
Video verification
Postcard (sometimes)

Step 9: Complete Profile

Add:

Business hours
Photos
Logo
Description
Services/products
Step 10: Publish and Manage

After verification, your business becomes visible on Google Search and Google Maps.



You can then:
Reply to reviews
Upload updates
Check customer insights
Manage ratings
